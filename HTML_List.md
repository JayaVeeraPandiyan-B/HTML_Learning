Order List - list ku numbers la venum na ithu use panalam

  <ol>
    <li>One</li>
    <li>Two</li>
    <li>Three</li>
  </ol>

  Ithuku Output ipdi varum

    1. One
    2. Two
    3. Three

Unorder List - Ithuvum list tha but ithu point la varum

  <ul>
    <li>One</li>
    <li>Two</li>
    <li>Three</li>
  </ul>

  Ithuku Output ipdi varum

    . One
    . Two
    . Three
